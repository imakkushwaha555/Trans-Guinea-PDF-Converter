# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Trans-Guinea PDF Converter a
welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
orientation.

We expect all contributors, maintainers, users, and community members to act
professionally and in good faith.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on the issue, code, or documentation rather than the person.
- Respecting differing opinions, experiences, and communication styles.
- Protecting other people's privacy and not posting private documents,
  passwords, or personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and deliberate disruption.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or other inappropriate conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional open-source community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Please report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer through
the contact method listed in the repository's security or maintainer
information. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details.

Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. The project
maintainer may remove comments or contributions, close discussions, restrict
participation, or take other action considered appropriate to protect the
community.

We will not retaliate against anyone who makes a good-faith report.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests,
discussions, releases, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone
is officially representing the project in public spaces.
